# LinkedIn Kurallari

## Format
- Ilk 2 satir **hook**; "see more" ten once merak/deger vermeli.
- Kisa paragraflar (1-2 cumle), bol bosluk. 120-220 kelime ideal.
- Tek fikir, tek CTA. Emoji en fazla 2-3, anlam tasiyorsa.
- Hashtag: 3-5 adet, sonda (ornek: #DataScience #MachineLearning #Python #LLM #BuildInPublic).
- Harici link ilk yorumda veya post sonunda; mumkunse gorsel/carousel ekle.

## Hook Kaliplari
1. **Sonuc:** "I cut inference latency by 63% with one change."
2. **Hata:** "My first model looked perfect. It was leaking data."
3. **Soru:** "Can a small RAG system beat a keyword search?"
4. **Liste:** "3 things I learned building a FastAPI ML service."
Her post icin 2 alternatif hook uret.

## CTA Kaliplari
- "Repo link in comments - feedback welcome."
- "What would you try next?"
- "If you work on X, I'd love to hear your approach."

## Lansman Post Iskeleti
```
<Hook>

<Problem in 1-2 lines>

What I built:
- <point 1>
- <point 2>
- <point 3>

Result: <measured outcome, honest>

<Lesson or limitation>

<CTA>

#tags
```

## Ton
- Ogrenen, ureten, paylasan muhendis. "Expert" iddiasi yok; "I built, I measured, I learned".
- Ekip veya veri kaynagi katkisi varsa tesekkur et.

## Paylasim Ritmi (Oneri)
- Haftada 2-3 post: 1 proje lansmani, 1 ogrenim notu, 1 etkilesim sorusu.
- Yayindan sonraki ilk saatte yorumlara cevap ver.

## Yasaklar
- Sahte veya olculmemis metrik, "guaranteed", "secret trick" tonu.
- Engagement bait ("comment YES if...") ve spam etiketleme.
- Gizli veya kisisel veri.
